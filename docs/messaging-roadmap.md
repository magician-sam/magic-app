# Backstage messaging — requested 5 October 2026

After the private WhatsApp archive import, add a Messaging workspace:

- SMS and WhatsApp composer for one customer or a selected customer audience.
- Transactional reminders and event updates; public-show invitations; app invitations; offers; seasonal announcements; rebooking and birthday reminders.
- Templates with customer/event placeholders and a preview before sending.
- Audience filters by verified customer, service interest, past events, locality, language and contact permission; selected audience count and exclusions.
- Marketing consent/unsubscribe and do-not-contact enforcement. Unverified imported chats are review candidates, not a mailing list.
- Saved drafts, scheduled campaigns, reminder rules, cancellation and duplicate-send protection.
- Per-recipient delivery/failure history, retry controls, and staff/device audit history.
- Roles: approved staff can draft; owner controls sending and campaigns.
- Record customer replies in their contact history when a supported provider supplies them.
- Provider connection status and test delivery before activating automation. A WhatsApp Business phone account alone does not establish API messaging access.

Do not send invitations automatically as part of importing historical chats. Integration setup and actual sends are separate actions.
