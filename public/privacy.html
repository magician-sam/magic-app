Privacy & your data

Your celebration. Your details.

This page explains how the current Magic App uses customer information. Updated 13 October 2026. For questions, contact Sam at sam.wehbi@gmail.com.

Information used to arrange your event

Account and enquiry details can include your name, phone number, optional email, event date and venue, service choices, messages or notes, and the proposal, payment and event history recorded by the team. If you previously contacted the business, it may retain your contact details and short enquiry or event notes for customer follow-up.

Only share information needed to arrange the event. Avoid putting sensitive information about guests or children into general notes when a private conversation would be more appropriate.

Who can use it

Authorized business staff use records to prepare proposals, coordinate services and manage events. Assigned artists receive the job details made available through their permissions. Authorized platform support can access records to help operate the app; support access is logged.

The website uses hosting, database and file-storage services to operate. Email, notification and external communication services also process the information needed for their functions when those features are used. Some processing may take place outside Lebanon.

Sign-in and saved plans

Sign-in uses a session cookie. The app also uses browser storage to keep event selections and preferences. Choosing “Save for later” keeps a plan on that device for 30 days; “Forget saved copy” removes that saved copy. Clearing browser data also removes local selections but does not delete records already sent to the business.

Shared event-plan links contain show choices. A private event link can give access to a proposal and event details. Keep private links confidential.

Learning what visitors enjoy

The app counts page visits by source and clicks on shows, services and occasions. These app reports use daily totals rather than names or contact details. Hosting and security systems may separately process technical request information, such as an IP address or browser details, to run and protect the service.

Reviews, photos and messages

Public customer reviews require publication permission. A photo attached to a review has a separate permission choice. Public portfolio images are separate from private event records. Contact us if a photo or review needs attention.

Marketing permission is optional. Contact the team to change your communication preferences. Browser or phone notification permission can also be managed in your device settings.

Opening WhatsApp or another external service takes you to that service, which has its own privacy practices. Review a prefilled message before sending it.

Corrections, access and removal requests

Email sam.wehbi@gmail.com to ask about your records, request a correction or discuss deletion or publication permission. Include enough information to find your account or event; do not send a password. We may need to verify that the request relates to you.

Retention depends on the record’s purpose, event or financial obligations and backup arrangements. Ask the team about the records involved in your request. A deletion request is reviewed; it does not silently change agreed event or payment records.

Opening the map loads OpenStreetMap tiles. Your browser contacts the map provider to display the area you view.
