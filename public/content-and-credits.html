Something needs our attention?

If you own rights to material shown here, appear in it, or represent a child shown in it, email sam.wehbi@gmail.com.

  • Include the page link and identify the photograph, video or wording.
  • Explain your connection to it and what you would like reviewed.
  • Provide a way to contact you. Share supporting permission or ownership evidence if appropriate.

Please do not send passwords or unnecessary identity documents. The team will review the concern and determine the appropriate correction or removal.
