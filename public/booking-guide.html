Booking guide

Good moments start with clear plans.

A little imagination, a few practical details and a proposal you can review. Here’s how to plan with Magic App.

  1. Gather your favourites

    Explore the full collection and add shows or services to your event box. Your choices are a shortlist; adding an item does not reserve it.

  2. Tell us about your occasion

    Create an account or sign in before sending your event request. Your selected shows stay with you. Share your date, location and the details that matter to your guests.

  3. Shape the proposal together

    The event team checks availability, venue needs and the services requested. Review the named performers or providers, what is included, the price, any transport or setup charges and the payment agreement.

  4. Wait for your final confirmation

    A request, saved plan or gallery photo is not a confirmed booking. Check the confirmation in your event page and the arrangements agreed with the team.

One occasion, different talents

Some experiences are performed by Sam; others involve guest artists or independent businesses. Your proposal should make clear who is providing each service and who to contact. Playground and agency enquiries are reviewed individually; a show selection does not include venue entry unless it is expressly agreed.

Before you say yes

  • Check the date, location, services and contact person.
  • Agree any deposit, balance and payment timing.
  • Ask which cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions apply to your proposal.
  • Share access needs, allergies or venue restrictions relevant to the services you choose.

Let the team know if your plans change. Dates, performers and venue arrangements may need to be checked again.

Photos are the beginning of the conversation

Gallery images show past work or presentation ideas. They do not guarantee an identical performer, costume, design, setup or venue. The details for your occasion are agreed in your proposal.

Have a question about your plan? Contact the Magic App team or use WhatsApp from the show you are exploring.
